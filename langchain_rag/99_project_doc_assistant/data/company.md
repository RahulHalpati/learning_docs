# About Acme

Acme is a small e-commerce company founded in 2019 and headquartered in
**Berlin, Germany**. We sell home-office equipment to customers in over 40
countries.

Our customer support team is available Monday to Friday, 9am–6pm CET. You can
reach support by email at help@acme.example or through the chat widget on our
website.

Acme is privately owned and employs about 50 people.

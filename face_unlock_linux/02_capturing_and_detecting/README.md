# Section 02 · Capturing & detecting

> **Prerequisites:** [01 · Foundations](../01_foundations/README.md) · **Time:** ~45 min

The first two steps of the pipeline: get an image (webcam or file) and **find the face** in it with OpenCV. No recognition yet — just "where is the face?".

## Modules

| # | Module | The question it answers |
|---|--------|-------------------------|
| 02-1 | [Webcam capture](01_webcam_capture.md) | How do I grab frames from the camera (and work from files offline)? |
| 02-2 | [Face detection](02_face_detection.md) | How do I locate a face in an image with a Haar cascade? |

## What you'll be able to do after this section

- Read images and capture webcam frames with OpenCV.
- Detect faces with a Haar cascade and get bounding boxes.
- Run detection offline on the bundled sample image (no camera).

# Section 04 · Ethical Hacking Methodology

> A random collection of attacks isn't a pentest — it's noise. The methodology gives you
> a structured, repeatable process: find everything, exploit what matters, explain how to
> fix it. This section teaches the full lifecycle.

> ⚠️ **LEGAL REMINDER:** Real-world application of this methodology requires explicit written authorisation. In this course, all exercises target the local lab.

| Module | What you'll learn | Time |
|---|---|---|
| [01 Legal & ethical framework](01_legal_and_ethical_framework.md) | Authorisation, scope, rules of engagement, responsible disclosure | ~1 h |
| [02 Reconnaissance & OSINT](02_reconnaissance_osint.md) | Passive recon, WHOIS, Google dorks, theHarvester, Shodan concepts | ~1.5 h |
| [03 Scanning & enumeration](03_scanning_and_enumeration.md) | Structured nmap workflow, banner grabbing, building a target map | ~1.5 h |
| [04 Exploitation basics](04_exploitation_basics.md) | CVE/CWE, finding and using exploits, safe exploitation in the lab | ~1.5 h |
| [05 Post-exploitation & pivoting](05_post_exploitation_and_pivoting.md) | Maintaining access, lateral movement, covering tracks | ~1 h |
| [06 Reporting & remediation](06_reporting_and_remediation.md) | CVSS scoring, pentest report structure, writing findings | ~0.5 h |

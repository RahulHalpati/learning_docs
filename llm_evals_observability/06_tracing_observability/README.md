# Section 06 · Tracing & observability

> **Prerequisites:** [01 · Foundations](../01_foundations/README.md) · **Time:** ~2.5 h

Evals tell you how good the system is before you ship. **Observability** tells you what it's doing right now — every step, every token, every cent, every millisecond.

- Attribute tokens, cost, and latency per span and per request.
- Move to Langfuse/OTel without rewriting your instrumentation.

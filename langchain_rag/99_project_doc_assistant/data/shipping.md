# Shipping

We ship to most countries worldwide. Standard shipping takes **3–5 business days**
within the continental US and is **free on orders over $50**. Orders under $50
have a flat $4.99 shipping fee.

Express shipping (1–2 business days) is available at checkout for $14.99.
International orders take 7–21 business days depending on the destination and
local customs processing.

Once an order ships, you receive a tracking link by email.

# Refunds and Returns

Customers may return most items within **30 days** of purchase for a full refund,
provided they have the original receipt. Items must be unused and in their
original packaging.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5–7 business days to
appear. Gift cards and downloadable software are **non-refundable**.

To start a return, email returns@acme.example with your order number.

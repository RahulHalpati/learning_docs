# 03 · Filters

> **Level:** Intermediate · **Prerequisites:** [02 · Core operations](../02_core_operations/README.md)
> **Time:** ~2.5 hours

Filters are where ffmpeg stops being a converter and becomes an editor. Everything visual — resizing, cropping, reframing, overlays, colour, blur, speed — is a filter, and they all share one syntax.

This section is the highest-leverage part of the course. Once filtergraphs make sense, the ffmpeg documentation becomes usable, because that's the language it's written in.

| # | Module | You'll be able to… |
|---|--------|--------------------|
| 1 | [Filtergraph syntax](01_filtergraphs.md) | Read and write chains, labels, and `-filter_complex` |
| 2 | [Scale, crop & vertical video](02_scale_crop_vertical.md) | Resize safely; reframe landscape to 9:16 three different ways |
| 3 | [Text & overlays](03_drawtext_overlays.md) | Burn text, timestamps, logos, and progress bars into video |

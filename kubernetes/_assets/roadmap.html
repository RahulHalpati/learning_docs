Docker→ CI/CD→ OpenTofu→ Kubernetes→ AWS / LocalStack

Two real gotchas this course caught: kubectl apply -f dir/ is alphabetical, so manifests need 00-/10- prefixes or the namespace applies too late; and a port-forward on a busy host port fails silently and your curl hits the wrong app. Both surfaced by reading the output, not assuming it.
